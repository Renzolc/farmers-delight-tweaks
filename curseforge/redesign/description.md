## 🔄 THE DECRAFTER MOVED TO DISASSEMBLY DELIGHT

![The Decrafter moved to Disassembly Delight in 1.1.0](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Renzolc/farmers-delight-tweaks/main/curseforge/redesign/media-0-decrafter-moved.png)

**Since 1.1.0, the Decrafter and the Decrafter Upgrade are no longer part of Farmer's Delight Tweaks.** They moved to a separate mod, **[Disassembly Delight](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/disassembly-delight)**, where they are now the **Disassembly Table** and the **Disassembly Table Upgrade**.

**Updating a world that has Decrafters, or Decrafter Upgrades in backpacks:**

1. Back up your world.
2. Install **[Disassembly Delight](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/disassembly-delight) 1.0.0 or newer together with Farmer's Delight Tweaks 1.1.0 or newer** before you open the world.
3. Open the world. Placed Decrafters keep their contents and become Disassembly Tables, and Decrafter Upgrades stay in their backpacks as Disassembly Table Upgrades.

⚠️ **If you update to 1.1.0 without Disassembly Delight, every Decrafter and Decrafter Upgrade in the world is deleted when it loads.** Farmer's Delight Tweaks logs a warning at startup when Disassembly Delight is missing. Disassembly Delight won't start next to Farmer's Delight Tweaks 1.0.x, so update both together.

## 📖 ABOUT

**Farmer's Delight Tweaks** is a Farmer's Delight add-on for NeoForge 1.21.1. It adds storage crates and sacks for the foods, seeds and saplings that Farmer's Delight doesn't cover, so a pantry wall holds nine stacks per block. 🧺

It also teaches the cutting board to take things apart: rope, canvas, nets, fences and knives go back to straw, sticks and nuggets, and Create parts and Sophisticated gear come apart when those mods are installed. A guard stops the board from cutting a container that still holds items. 🔪

The automatic machine that runs these recipes, the old Decrafter, now lives in **[Disassembly Delight](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/disassembly-delight)**. Each mod works without the other. 🔄

![divider](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Renzolc/farmers-delight-tweaks/main/curseforge/redesign/divider-farmers-delight-tweaks.png)

## 📚 FEATURES

- 🍎 **Crates:** Apple, sweet berry, glow berry, cocoa bean, sugar cane, nether wart and chorus fruit crates. 9 items in, 1 block.
- 🌱 **Seed sacks:** Wheat, pumpkin, melon, beetroot, torchflower, cabbage and tomato seeds.
- 🌳 **Sapling sacks:** Every vanilla sapling, plus the mangrove propagule.
- 🍇 **Modded crops:** Sacks and crates for Cultural, Veggies, Expanded, Fruits and Crabber's Delight, Quark's blossom and ancient saplings, and Supplementaries flax seeds. Their recipes only appear when that mod is installed.
- 🔪 **Cutting-board salvage:** Rope, canvas, safety nets, rope fences and knives come back apart on the cutting board.
- ⚙️ **Create parts:** Kinetics, machines, logistics parts, casings and tools salvage back to their main materials. Cheap bulk crafts like shafts are skipped.
- 🎒 **Sophisticated gear:** Backpacks, storage tiers and upgrades from Sophisticated Backpacks and Sophisticated Storage step back toward the previous tier.
- 🧩 **Pack compat:** Cutting recipes for Supplementaries fiber and decor, Quark rope, More Delight knives and Handcrafted sheets.
- 🛡️ **Cutting-board guard:** A Sophisticated chest or backpack, a Create toolbox, a shulker box or any other container that still holds items is never cut. Empty it first.
- 🥪 **Shared ingredients:** Tag bridges so Farmer's Delight add-ons accept each other's squid and calamari, cheese, bread slices, dough and cut vegetables.
- 📖 **Recipe book:** Crate and sack recipes unlock as soon as you pick up the ingredients.

## 📦 INSTALLATION

**⏩ INSTALL ON BOTH CLIENT AND SERVER (NEOFORGE 1.21.1)**

**⏩ REQUIRES [FARMER'S DELIGHT](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/farmers-delight) 1.2 OR NEWER**

**⏩ OPTIONAL: [DISASSEMBLY DELIGHT](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/disassembly-delight) 1.0.0 OR NEWER, FOR THE DISASSEMBLY TABLE (THE OLD DECRAFTER)**

**⏩ OPTIONAL: [CREATE](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/create), [SOPHISTICATED BACKPACKS](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/sophisticated-backpacks) 3.20+ AND [SOPHISTICATED STORAGE](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/sophisticated-storage), FOR THEIR SALVAGE RECIPES**

Updating from 1.0.x with Decrafters in your world? Follow the steps in **The Decrafter moved to Disassembly Delight** at the top of this page before you open the world.

## ⚙️ CONFIGURATION

There is no config file; nothing is created in `.minecraft/config/`. Crates, sacks and cutting-board recipes are ordinary datapack recipes under `data/fd_storage_compat/recipe/`, so a datapack can change or remove them. The cutting-board guard reads its rules from `fd_storage_compat/container_rules.json` inside the jar.

The full list of integrations and shared ingredient tags is in [`INTEGRATIONS.md` on GitHub](https://github.com/Renzolc/farmers-delight-tweaks/blob/main/INTEGRATIONS.md).
