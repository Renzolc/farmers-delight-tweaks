## 1.0.15

- Replaced Simple Uncrafter and Advanced Uncrafter with one Decrafter Upgrade.
- Craft: 4 leather + 1 Decrafter. Putting an item in the upgrade inserts the full craft ingredients into the backpack. If they do not fit, the input stays.
- Cutting board and Decrafter block still use partial salvage. Full counts are only through the Decrafter Upgrade, for every uncraft this mod already defines.
- The upgrade itself full-returns into 4 leather + 1 Decrafter only through another Decrafter Upgrade. The Decrafter block will not reverse-craft it.
- Fixed the backpack GUI crash (`Invalid stack minecraft:air`) caused by output slots rejecting the empty stacks Sophisticated Core syncs.
