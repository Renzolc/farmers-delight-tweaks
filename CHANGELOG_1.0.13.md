## 1.0.13

- Simple Uncrafter and Advanced Uncrafter, two optional Sophisticated Backpacks upgrades.
- Both use full-return uncrafts for every cutting recipe this mod already defines (base set plus 1.0.12 Sophisticated Backpacks / Storage and Create). The cutting board and Decrafter keep the existing partial yields.
- Simple: put the item in the upgrade slot; the full ingredients show up in the panel and you take them by hand.
- Advanced: same input and same full counts, but the ingredients are inserted into the backpack instead.
- The upgrade items themselves have full-return recipes only (no cutting-board recipe, and the Decrafter will not reverse-craft them). Uncrafting one requires another Uncrafter already installed.
